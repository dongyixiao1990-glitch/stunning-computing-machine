My Story

From Air Traffic Control to Quality Assurance

My journey into Software Quality Assurance began with a unique foundation in Air Traffic Control from the Civil Aviation University of China. During my studies, I developed an exceptional eye for detail and the ability to analyze complex systems under pressure.

After completing my MBA at Wuhan University of Science and Technology, I transitioned into the world of technology, bringing my analytical mindset and precision-focused approach to software testing.

Quality is not an act, it is a habit. Every test case is an opportunity to ensure excellence.

Today, with 5+ years of experience at Applause working with Ant Group/Alipay+ and Nike, I have tested applications used by millions across APAC and EMEA regions.
